# Music library

Background beds for the daily shorts. **Public domain or CC0 only — both the
composition and the recording.** A performance can carry its own copyright
long after the composer's has expired, and a claimed or muted upload loses its
reach. Check `LicenseShortName` on Commons before adding anything.

The audio files are not committed (they are large and re-fetchable); this list
is the record of where each came from and why it is safe.
