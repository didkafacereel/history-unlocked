# Music library

Background beds for the daily shorts. **Public domain or CC0 only — both the
composition and the recording.** A performance can carry its own copyright
long after the composer's has expired, and a claimed or muted upload loses its
reach. Check `LicenseShortName` on Commons before adding anything.

The audio files are not committed (they are large and re-fetchable:
`node video/tools/fetch-music.mjs`, which re-checks each licence). The
machine-readable list, with moods, is `library.json`; this table is the
record of where each came from and why it is safe. All checked 25 Sep 2026.
