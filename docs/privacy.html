Privacy Policy

History Unlocked · last updated 15 September 2026

History Unlocked does not have accounts you must create, does not run analytics, and does not have a server that stores what you read. Almost everything the app knows about you is on your phone and goes away when you uninstall it.

What stays on your device

The app keeps the following in your phone's own app storage. It is never sent anywhere, and no one — including us — can read it.

What leaves your device

The archive itself. The app downloads a single file of historical events from our hosting and caches it. Like any web request, the server that serves it can see your IP address and the time of the request in its ordinary logs. Nothing in that request identifies you or says what you read.

Images. Event pictures and portraits are loaded directly from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). Wikimedia therefore sees your IP address when an image loads, under their privacy policy. We do not send them anything else.

Signing in with Google — optional. You do not need an account to read. If you choose to sign in, Google gives the app your name, email address and Google account ID. We use them only to attach your purchase to you so it survives a new phone. There is no profile, no mailing list, and no sharing with anyone.

Purchases. Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We never see or handle your card details. RevenueCat receives a purchase identifier and, if you signed in, your account ID — see the RevenueCat privacy policy and Google's.

Suggestions you send. If you use "Missing something from this day?", the app opens your own email app or a form with the text prefilled. Nothing is sent until you send it, and you can see and edit every word first.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No location access. Events carry coordinates; your phone's location is never requested.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No server-side record of what you read, answered or kept.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled by your phone, on your phone. There is no push server, so no one is notified that you were notified. Turning it off in the app, or in your phone's settings, stops it completely.

Children

History Unlocked is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them. It covers historical events including war and disaster, described plainly.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything listed under "What stays on your device" — permanently, and there is no copy elsewhere to request. If you signed in with Google, you can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have any account record deleted from our side, write to the address below and it will be done within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the app will say so before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
